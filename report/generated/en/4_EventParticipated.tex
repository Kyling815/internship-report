enterprise architectures, system operations, and security, Agentic AI.
These events helped me connect the project work with practical examples
of how Cloud and AI technologies are designed, deployed, and operated in
real environments.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\textbf{Event 1 - FCAJ Community Day: Data Driven, AI
Risen}\par

\textbf{Date:} June 27, 2026\\
\textbf{Participation:} Online

The event introduced practical applications of data-driven systems and
generative AI in enterprise environments. The main topics included AI
Agents, Amazon Q, Voice AI, DevOps automation, and security
considerations for Model Context Protocol integrations. I learned how AI
capabilities should be connected to reliable data sources, controlled
tools, and appropriate permission boundaries instead of being treated as
isolated demonstrations.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\textbf{Event 2 - AWS Study Tour: Enterprise Cloud Architectures
and Industry
Applications}\par

\textbf{Date:} May 23, 2026\\
\textbf{Participation:} Online

This study tour presented common patterns used to design production
workloads on AWS. The discussion covered network segmentation, managed
compute and storage, high availability, monitoring, security, and cost
awareness. The event helped me relate the Internship Application
Platform to enterprise architecture principles, especially the
separation of public and private resources, least-privilege access,
operational visibility, and failure recovery.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\textbf{Event 3 - FCAJ x Agentic AI Build Week
2026}\par

\textbf{Date:} July 26, 2026\\
\textbf{Participation:} Offline

The build week focused on developing Agentic AI applications through
rapid prototyping and teamwork. Core topics included Amazon Bedrock,
tool-using agents, workflow orchestration, prompt and context design,
and responsible access to external systems. The hands-on format
strengthened my understanding of how an AI feature moves from an initial
idea to a working application that can be tested, demonstrated, and
improved from user feedback.

The three events provided me with a more comprehensive understanding of
the process of developing Cloud and AI products, from problem
identification and architecture design to Agentic AI development,
deployment, monitoring, and system security.

Through the presented topics, I recognized that technical knowledge
represents only one part of product development. An effective solution
must also address actual needs, operate reliably, control costs, protect
data, and deliver practical value to users.

The knowledge gained from these events will help me improve my
internship project in a more structured and secure manner, particularly
in areas related to AWS architecture, AI Agents, monitoring, data
management, and system security.
