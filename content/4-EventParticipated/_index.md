During my internship, I followed three events related to AWS Cloud, enterprise architectures, system operations, and security, Agentic AI. These events helped me connect the project work with practical examples of how Cloud and AI technologies are designed, deployed, and operated in real environments.

| No. | Event | Time | Participation Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | FCAJ Community Day – June 2026: Data Driven, AI Risen | June 27, 2026 | Online |
| 2 | AWS Study Tour: Enterprise Cloud Architectures and Industry Applications | May 23, 2026 | Online |
| 3 | FCAJ x Agentic AI Build Week 2026 | July 26, 2026 | Offline |

### Event 1 - FCAJ Community Day: Data Driven, AI Risen

**Date:** June 27, 2026\
**Participation:** Online

The event introduced practical applications of data-driven systems and generative AI in enterprise environments. The main topics included AI Agents, Amazon Q, Voice AI, DevOps automation, and security considerations for Model Context Protocol integrations. I learned how AI capabilities should be connected to reliable data sources, controlled tools, and appropriate permission boundaries instead of being treated as isolated demonstrations.

### Event 2 - AWS Study Tour: Enterprise Cloud Architectures and Industry Applications

**Date:** May 23, 2026\
**Participation:** Online

This study tour presented common patterns used to design production workloads on AWS. The discussion covered network segmentation, managed compute and storage, high availability, monitoring, security, and cost awareness. The event helped me relate the Internship Application Platform to enterprise architecture principles, especially the separation of public and private resources, least-privilege access, operational visibility, and failure recovery.

### Event 3 - FCAJ x Agentic AI Build Week 2026

**Date:** July 26, 2026\
**Participation:** Offline

The build week focused on developing Agentic AI applications through rapid prototyping and teamwork. Core topics included Amazon Bedrock, tool-using agents, workflow orchestration, prompt and context design, and responsible access to external systems. The hands-on format strengthened my understanding of how an AI feature moves from an initial idea to a working application that can be tested, demonstrated, and improved from user feedback.

The three events provided me with a more comprehensive understanding of the process of developing Cloud and AI products, from problem identification and architecture design to Agentic AI development, deployment, monitoring, and system security.

Through the presented topics, I recognized that technical knowledge represents only one part of product development. An effective solution must also address actual needs, operate reliably, control costs, protect data, and deliver practical value to users.

The knowledge gained from these events will help me improve my internship project in a more structured and secure manner, particularly in areas related to AWS architecture, AI Agents, monitoring, data management, and system security.
